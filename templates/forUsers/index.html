Track

The feature enables users to monitor the prices of products they are interested in and receive notifications when there are changes, such as price drops or increases. This feature empowers users to make informed purchasing decisions and take advantage of favorable pricing opportunities..


Request

The request feature enables users to submit requests, inquiries, complaints, or feedback directly to service providers through the platform. This feature acts as a bridge between users and service providers, facilitating seamless communication and improving overall customer experience.


Discount predict

Verify the accuracy of predicted discounts for products with price fluctuations, comparing them against actual discount events. Conduct sensitivity analysis to evaluate prediction accuracy under different factors such as product popularity, seasonality, and competitor pricing, ensuring robust predictive capabilities.

Date predict

Validate the accuracy of predicted availability dates for pre-order products, ensuring alignment with actual release dates. Assess the confidence level of date predictions across various product scenarios, instilling user trust in the system's predictive capabilities.
